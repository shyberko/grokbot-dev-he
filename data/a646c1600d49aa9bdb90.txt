# grokbot.dev

> Open-source directory of ready-to-use Grok Bot prompts, plugins, collections, and news.
> Browse it, or point your own Grok Bot at the JSON API below and it pulls new
> entries on your schedule.

## Start here
- [Agent contract](https://grokbot.dev/agent/): copy-paste instructions for a Grok Bot to connect and sync
- [Feed JSON](https://grokbot.dev/api/v1/feed.json): RECOMMENDED - the complete lean list (plugins, use cases, collections, and news), newest first. News items have type "news" plus kind, important, and external_url.
- [Templates JSON](https://grokbot.dev/api/v1/templates.json): Shareable Bots - Grok Bot templates people shared on X. Each carries share_url, the "Add to Grok Bot" install link, plus tags and the sharer credit. Full item at /api/v1/templates/<slug>.json.
- [News JSON](https://grokbot.dev/api/v1/news.json): releases, deals, updates, and announcements. Full item at /api/v1/news/<slug>.json.
- [Status JSON](https://grokbot.dev/api/v1/status.json): API version, capabilities, notices, deprecations, changelog. Poll it to learn if the API changed; v1 is additive-only and every response carries a schema_revision.
- [Per-entry detail](https://grokbot.dev/api/v1/use-cases/<slug>.json): full record for one entry (also /api/v1/plugins/<slug>.json)
- [Latest JSON](https://grokbot.dev/api/v1/latest.json): 50 newest, full records
- [RSS](https://grokbot.dev/rss.xml)

## Plugins
- [DC Hub](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/dc-hub/): Live data center, power, grid and fiber data for site selection, with citations.
- [CurlShot](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/curlshot/): Give your agent a screenshot API: any public URL as PNG, PDF or scrolling video.
- [UXKIN](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/uxkin/): Real app screens and user journeys your Grok Bot checks before it designs any UI
- [Jetpost](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/jetpost/): Tell your agent "make a note." Your team, and their agents, pick it up from there.
- [Employed](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/employed/): Give your Grok Bot live jobs from 5,000 employer career sites, plus your job tracker
- [Shareout](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/shareout/): Share HTML proposals, reports and prototypes as browser links with feedback and revisions
- [BulkTranscripts](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/bulktranscripts/): Let your Grok Bot read whole YouTube channels and playlists as transcripts.
- [Fonino: a real iPhone for your Grok Bot](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/fonino/): A real, non-jailbroken iPhone your Grok Bot can tap, type and swipe in, with live takeover
- [RawTree](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/rawtree/): Give your Grok Bot a schema-free analytics database it can write events to and query.
- [Upload-Post](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/upload-post/): Let your agent publish, schedule and analyze posts on 11 social networks.
- [WhatSetter](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/whatsetter/): Run your WhatsApp AI setter from the agent: inbox, leads, bookings, campaigns
- [Kampalo](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/kampalo/): Brief synced Google and Meta ads, then pause a weak campaign only after you confirm.
- [BlindOracle (read-only)](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/blindoracle-marketplace-kit/): Check any agent's reputation and verify a settlement proof — free reads, it can't spend.
- [ZenSched](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/zensched/): Field workforce scheduling over MCP — shifts, GPS check-in, timesheets.
- [CentricMem](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/centricmem/): Hosted librarian so every agent cites the same Markdown cards.
- [AnalyticLunch](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/analyticlunch/): Give your Grok Bot your site traffic, UTM link builder and weekly competitor intel.
- [Jobkeepr](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/jobkeepr/): Give your Grok Bot your field service business: jobs, schedule, estimates, invoices.
- [Gojiberry AI](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/gojiberry/): Find warm B2B leads, manage outreach campaigns, and work your inbox from the agent
- [Prism Network](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/prism-network/): Live GPU capacity, per-second prices, and a public receipt for every run.
- [treg.to](https://grokbot.dev/plugins/treg-to/): 2,745 external APIs your Grok Bot can call with no provider keys, at the provider's rate
